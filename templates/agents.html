Why One Agent Is Not Enough

🧠💥

Context Overload

Single agents struggle with massive codebases and complex requirements. They lose focus and make suboptimal decisions when handling everything at once.

👁️‍🗨️

Bias & Blind Spots

One perspective leads to tunnel vision. Critical security flaws, performance issues, and architectural problems slip through unchecked.

📈⚠️

Scaling Limitations

As your team grows, a single agent becomes a bottleneck. Quality degrades and delivery slows when one system handles all development tasks.
