About the CTO Breakfast Club

Building bridges between technology leaders, one breakfast at a time

Our Mission 🎯

The CTO Breakfast Club by Microsoft is a vibrant community dedicated to bringing together Chief Technology Officers and senior technology leaders from around the world. We believe that the best ideas emerge when brilliant minds gather in an informal, engaging environment.

Our mission is to foster meaningful connections, facilitate knowledge sharing, and create opportunities for collaboration among technology leaders who are shaping the future of their organizations.

What We Do ☕

  • 🍳 Monthly Breakfast Meetups: Casual gatherings in major cities worldwide
  • 💻 Virtual Roundtables: Online discussions on trending tech topics
  • 🎤 Guest Speakers: Industry leaders sharing insights and experiences
  • 🔬 Tech Deep Dives: Explore emerging technologies and best practices
  • 🤝 Peer Mentoring: Connect with experienced CTOs for guidance
  • 📊 Leadership Workshops: Develop skills in team building and strategy
