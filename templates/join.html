Frequently Asked Questions 🤔

Who can join?

The club is open to CTOs, VPs of Engineering, Technical Directors, and senior technology leaders from all industries.

Is there a membership fee?

Membership is free! As a Microsoft initiative, we want to make this accessible to all technology leaders.

How often are meetings?

We host monthly in-person breakfasts in major cities and bi-weekly virtual roundtables that you can join from anywhere.

What if I can't attend in person?

No problem! Our virtual events allow you to participate from anywhere, and all content is available in our members area.

Do I need to use Microsoft products?

Not at all! While we're powered by Microsoft, members use a wide variety of technologies. We welcome all tech leaders.

How do I get involved?

Simply fill out the form above! Once approved, you'll receive access to our community platform and event invitations.
